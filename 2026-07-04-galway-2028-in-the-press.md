The confirmation that Galway will host the European Masters Athletics Championships Non-Stadia in May 2028 has generated great coverage across Irish media and the wider athletics community. We're delighted to see the word spreading!

## What they're saying

**Galway Advertiser** (2 July 2026) led the local coverage, quoting Former Mayor of Galway Cllr Mike Cubbard:

> "This is a fantastic achievement for Galway and a proud moment for our city. The Championships will bring thousands of athletes and visitors to Galway, showcasing our community spirit, our coastal setting and our ability to deliver major international events."

Sally-Ann O'Brien, Head of Tourism at Galway City Council, called it a "strategically important win for Galway", adding: "Participation events like this deliver real economic impact, extend the tourism season and highlight Galway as a world-class destination for sporting events."
