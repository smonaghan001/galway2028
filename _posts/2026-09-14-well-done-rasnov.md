The European Masters Off Road Running Championships (EMORRC) concluded this weekend in Râșnov, Romania, and what a championship it was.

Over three days, more than 400 masters athletes from across Europe took on the mountains of Transylvania — the Vertical Race, Long Distance, and Classic Up&Down — in what organisers Run Transylvania delivered as a seamless, atmospheric and genuinely memorable event. Stunning scenery, warm hospitality and serious competition: everything a masters athletics championship should be.

From the Galway 2028 organising team, a heartfelt congratulations to everyone involved in making EMORRC 2026 such a success — the organising committee, the volunteers, and the people of Râșnov who welcomed so many athletes and their families to their beautiful town.

Events like this are an inspiration as we build towards the European Masters Athletics Championships Non-Stadia here in Galway in May 2028. We hope many of the athletes who raced in Romania this weekend will be lining up on the Wild Atlantic Way in two years' time.

Maith sibh go léir — well done to all!

*The EMACNS Galway 2028 Organising Committee*
