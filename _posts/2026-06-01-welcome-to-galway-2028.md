We are thrilled to officially launch the website for EMACNS Galway 2028 — the European Masters Athletics Championships Non-Stadia, coming to Galway, Ireland in May 2028.

Planning is well underway and we look forward to welcoming masters athletes from over 20 nations to one of Ireland's most vibrant and beautiful cities.

Over the coming months we will be sharing updates on the event programme, venues, accommodation and everything you need to know to make the most of your time in Galway.

Stay tuned — and if you'd like to volunteer or discuss sponsorship opportunities, please visit the relevant sections of this website.

*The EMACNS Galway 2028 Organising Committee*
